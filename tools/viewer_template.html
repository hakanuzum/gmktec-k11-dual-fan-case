    What to buy

    • 2 × 120 mm fans, 25 mm thick. Example: Noctua NF-F12 PWM (12 V, 4-pin). Any standard 120 mm fan fits; the hole spacing is always 105 mm.
    • USB 5 V→12 V step-up fan cable (USB-A male → 4-pin fan, e.g. Akasa AK-CBFA10-60BK). It must say "5V→12V" or "step-up"; a plain USB fan cable gives 5 V and will not spin a 12 V fan. An NF-F12 draws at most 0.05 A; the Y-cable in the fan box lets two fans share one cable.
    • 8 × M3×10 countersunk hex socket screws (DIN 7991), black. No washers; tightened with a 2 mm hex key. The handles need no screws.
    • Black PETG filament. PLA can soften in the warm air leaving the rear.

    USB carries no PWM signal, so the fan runs at full speed (NF-F12: 1500 rpm, 22.4 dB(A)). For less noise, put the Low-Noise Adaptor (L.N.A.) from the fan box in line.

    Automated checks

    ✓At a K11 thickness of 32, 44 and 56 mm: no case part or handle intersects the K11 body or the fans; the front and rear port zones are completely clear.
    ✓All 8 countersunk plate screws and 8 fan screws line up with their holes at every thickness; the handle flanges sit on the holders.
    ✓The same checks pass with the 15 mm slim-fan setting.
    ✓Every printed part is a single watertight solid; the largest is 142 × 139 × 34 mm and fits a 180 mm bed.

    Collar and fan-pocket dimensions come from orenaksakal/gmktec-k11-noctua-mod, where they were fit-tested on a real K11. The K11 is a stand-in model; port positions are approximate. Source: github.com/hakanuzum/gmktec-k11-dual-fan-case
